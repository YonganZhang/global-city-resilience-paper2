# Data licence

The derived data in this repository (city results, council indicator ratings,
intermediate tables, analysis tables and figure source data) are released under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The derived data build on the following upstream sources, which remain under
their own terms: GHSL Urban Centre Database R2024A (European Commission, Joint
Research Centre; CC BY 4.0), the Global Infrastructure Risk Model and Resilience
Index (GIRI; UNEP-GRID and CDRI), WorldPop population density (CC BY 4.0) and
NASA Black Marble VIIRS night-time lights.
